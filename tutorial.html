00 / THE BIG IDEA

BUILD WITH BLOCKS.
WATCH IT APPEAR.

Build on the real board, and Build It Live shows the same shape on the screen.

A tiny computer under the board notices where your blocks are. It sends that information to the web app, which makes a matching 3D version. That matching screen version is called a digital twin.

08 / WHERE THINGS LIVE

MEET THE 4 MAIN AREAS

You do not need to remember every button. Start by knowing what each big area is for.

BUILD IT LIVEYOUR 3D WORKSPACE
01BUILD KITChoose blocks and files
02LIVE DATASee your numbers
03FEEDBACKSee what happened

01

Build Kit is your toolbox. Choose what kind of block to add, erase blocks, and open or save projects.

02

Live Data is your scoreboard. It shows the grid size, the model height, and how many blocks you used.

03

Feedback is the app talking to you. Look here when something works, fails, or needs your attention.

04

Control Deck changes how you build and look at the model, including Smooth view, Continuous Build, Autosave, and Layer View.
